# Product Requirement Document (PRD): BiteRush

## 1. Document Overview
* **Product Name:** BiteRush
* **Author:** Olayemi Olasunkanmi (Product Manager)
* **Status:** MVP Published

---

## 2. Target Personas
* **Primary:** Nigerian university students residing in campus hostels who order food 3–5 times a week on tight budgets.
* **Secondary:** Local campus food vendors seeking bulk order volumes during peak hours.
* **Tertiary:** Student Walkers (peer couriers) managing last-mile deliveries from common rooms.

---

## 3. Epics & Functional Requirements

### Epic 1: Batch Ordering System
* **FR-1.1:** System MUST calculate and display batch countdown timers (e.g., "Closes in 04:32").
* **FR-1.2:** System MUST automatically pool orders per hostel block to trigger discounted delivery tiers.

### Epic 2: CraveCash Wallet & Split Billing
* **FR-2.1:** Users MUST be able to fund their wallet via card or instant bank transfer.
* **FR-2.2:** Users MUST be able to split a cart balance with another verified student account.

### Epic 3: Student Verification
* **FR-3.1:** New sign-ups MUST provide a valid `.edu.ng` email address or upload student ID media for verification.

---

## 4. Key Metrics & Success Criteria
* **Primary KPI:** Conversion rate on batch orders vs. single orders.
* **Retention Metric:** 30-day repeat order rate for Naira-Saver plan subscribers.
* **Operational Metric:** Average delivery time under 15 minutes from vendor pickup to common-room drop-off.
