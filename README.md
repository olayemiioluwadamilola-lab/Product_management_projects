🚀 Featured Projects
1. BiteRush — Campus Food Delivery Platform
Tagline: Hot food. Lecture-hall fast. Hostel cheap.

Live Demo: BiteRush Live MVP

Role: Lead Product Manager & UI/UX Designer

Toolkit: Figma, Lovable, Notion, Jira

Key Focus: Hyper-local logistics, user retention, cost optimization for African higher education markets.

Overview
BiteRush is a 0-to-1 food delivery web platform conceptualized and built for Nigerian university students. It addresses core campus friction points: high individual delivery fees, irregular delivery times, and common-room drop-off confusion.

Core PM Highlights & Solutions
Hostel Batching Logic: Engineered a scheduled delivery window model allowing students in the same hostel to pool orders, cutting delivery fees by up to 80%.

CraveCash Wallet: Designed a frictionless 2-tap checkout system supporting instant bill-splitting between roommates to reduce cart abandonment.

Naira-Saver Subscriptions: Developed scheduled meal plan models aligned with student lecture timetables to lock in recurring revenue and weekly user retention.

Landmark Drop-offs: Optimized delivery routing by replacing vague address inputs with designated campus landmarks and common-room drop-off points.
