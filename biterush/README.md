# Case Study: BiteRush — Campus Food Delivery Platform

## 📌 Executive Summary
BiteRush is a 0-to-1 hyper-local food delivery platform built specifically for the constraints and behavior of Nigerian university campus environments. By addressing high delivery costs and delivery location ambiguity, BiteRush offers a targeted solution for student dining logistics.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
* **High Delivery Costs:** Standard food delivery apps charge individual delivery fees per order, making single meals unaffordable for average student budgets.
* **Delivery Friction:** Drivers struggle with informal campus navigation, leading to lost packages, cold food, and continuous phone calls to locate student hostels.
* **Payment Inefficiency:** Frequent bank transfer failures during peak hours slow down checkout times.

---

## 💡 Solution & Core Features
* **Hostel Batching Logic:** Schedules recurring delivery windows per hostel, pooling orders so students split delivery fees by up to 80%.
* **Common-Room Drop-offs:** Replaces specific room navigation with designated campus landmarks and common rooms.
* **CraveCash Wallet:** Enables a 2-tap pre-funded wallet checkout and instant bill-splitting between roommates.
* **Naira-Saver Plans:** Weekly subscription meal plans aligned with lecture timetables for predictable user retention.
